Green Ways Group installs suspended ceiling systems for commercial interiors, including exposed grid, concealed MF and other specified ceiling build-ups, coordinated with partitions, lighting, access points and building services.

Green Ways Group installs structural framing systems (SFS) in light-gauge steel to issued project designs, forming precise internal and external wall zones coordinated with the primary structure, boards, insulation and façade interfaces.

Green Ways Group installs commercial partitions, plasterboard linings and plastering packages, coordinating metal studs, boards, insulation, openings, service zones and specified fire, acoustic, moisture and finish requirements.
