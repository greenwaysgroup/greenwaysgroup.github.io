Commercial delivery / package control

THE WORK IS ONLY AS STRONG AS THE INFORMATION AROUND IT.

Commercial interior systems are connected work. The ceiling, framing or partition may be one package, but its edges, openings, services, performance and programme are shared with the wider project.

Scope definition[01]

Know exactly what belongs in the package.

Drawings, specifications, schedules and exclusions need to describe one coherent package before installation starts. Undefined scope becomes an interface problem later.

Interfaces[02]

Resolve where systems meet.

Ceilings, framing, partitions, services, doors, glazing and finishes share edges and openings. Coordination is strongest when those junctions are explicit before they are concealed.

Performance[03]

Keep requirements attached to the full build-up.

Fire, acoustic, moisture and duty requirements depend on the specified system and its details. Installation should follow the issued evidence rather than generic assumptions.

Sequence[04]

Make the work buildable in the programme.

Access, preceding trades, inspections, service coordination and following finishes all affect sequence. Useful programme information turns a drawing package into an installable one.

Delivery sequence

FROM PACKAGE DEFINITION TO INSTALLATION.

The sequence is deliberately simple: establish what is being built, coordinate the interfaces that affect it, then install against issued project information.

Step[01]

Define the package

Confirm the relevant drawings, specification, scope, programme and responsibility boundaries before mobilisation.

Step[02]

Coordinate the interfaces

Review setting-out, openings, services, adjoining systems and changes while they can still be resolved clearly.

Step[03]

Install to issued information

Build the agreed system, keep visible and concealed work aligned to the documented details, and raise unresolved change before it is buried.

FAQs / project review

USEFUL ANSWERS BEFORE FIRST CONTACT.

What type of work does Green Ways Group undertake?

GWG is a UK specialist subcontractor focused on suspended ceilings, structural framing systems (SFS), partitions, plasterboard linings and plastering within commercial interior and related construction packages.

Does GWG design the systems it installs?

Design responsibility is project-specific. GWG installs to the issued design and agreed scope. Structural design, system selection, performance evidence, warranty and other design duties remain with the party identified in the contract and project information.

What information is useful before discussing a package?

Send the project location, relevant package, tender or construction stage, programme dates and whatever drawings, specifications or schedules are available. A complete tender pack is helpful but not required for an initial relevance check.

How are fire and acoustic requirements treated?

Performance requirements are connected to the complete specified system. Board type, layers, insulation, framing, junctions, penetrations and other details need to match the relevant project information and approved evidence.
