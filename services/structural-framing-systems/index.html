Structural Framing Systems

Service definition

Light-gauge steel SFS installed to issued project designs, forming accurately set-out wall zones coordinated with the primary structure and adjoining build-ups.

What the package does

THE SYSTEM IN PROJECT CONTEXT.

Structural framing systems use light-gauge steel sections to form a precise framed construction. GWG installs SFS where it is included in the agreed package and suitable design information is issued, coordinating connections, openings and interfaces with the primary structure, boards, insulation and façade build-ups.

Package functions

What is installed

  • Light-gauge steel framing to documented wall zones and project geometry.
  • Accurate lines, openings and connection points for the specified assembly.
  • Coordination with floor edges, soffits, primary structure and adjoining systems.
  • Installation to issued structural and system information within the agreed responsibility boundary.

Information required

What needs to be defined

  • Coordinated SFS and structural drawings
  • Setting-out and connection details
  • Deflection and floor-edge information where applicable
  • Opening, sheathing, insulation and façade interfaces
  • Clear design, supply and warranty responsibilities

Key interfaces

What it has to meet

  • Primary structure
  • Sheathing
  • Insulation
  • Façade zones
  • Internal linings

Responsibility boundaryStructural design, engineering, system selection, supply, warranty and façade responsibility remain exactly as allocated by the contract and project team.
