What the package does

THE SYSTEM IN PROJECT CONTEXT.

A suspended ceiling creates a secondary ceiling plane beneath the structural soffit. Depending on the specified system, it can organise services, provide access to the void above and contribute to acoustic or fire-performance requirements. GWG installs the documented ceiling build-up within the agreed package and coordinates the visible and concealed interfaces around it.

Package functions

What is installed

  • Exposed grid, concealed MF and other documented ceiling systems.
  • Perimeters, levels, openings, access points and feature geometry shown in issued information.
  • Coordination with lighting, MEP services, partitions and adjoining finishes.
  • Installation to the specified build-up, line, level and finish requirements.

Information required

What needs to be defined

  • Reflected ceiling plans and setting-out information
  • Ceiling levels and system build-up
  • Perimeter, bulkhead and access details
  • Lighting, service and penetration coordination
  • Specified fire, acoustic and finish requirements

Key interfaces

What it has to meet

  • Partitions
  • MEP services
  • Lighting
  • Access panels
  • Perimeter details

Responsibility boundarySystem selection, performance criteria and interface responsibilities remain governed by the project drawings, specification and approved details.
