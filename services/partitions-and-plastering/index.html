Partitions and Plastering

What the package does

THE SYSTEM IN PROJECT CONTEXT.

A commercial drylining package can combine metal studs and tracks, insulation, plasterboard, wall linings and finishing around doors, glazing, service zones and adjoining construction. GWG installs partitions and plastering work to the documented layout, build-up and finish route within the agreed package.

Package functions

What is installed

  • Internal metal-stud partitions and wall linings to coordinated layouts.
  • Boarding, insulation, openings and service-zone coordination.
  • Straight, curved and feature construction where shown in the agreed scope.
  • Preparation for specified plaster, skim, tape-and-joint or other documented finishes.

Information required

What needs to be defined

  • Partition types, heights and setting-out
  • Board layers, insulation and duty requirements
  • Door, glazing and head-condition details
  • Service penetrations and coordinated openings
  • Finish schedules and documented performance requirements

Key interfaces

What it has to meet

  • Ceilings
  • Doors and glazing
  • MEP services
  • Floors
  • Finishes
