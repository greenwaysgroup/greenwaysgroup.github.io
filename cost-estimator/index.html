Why the range moves.

Specification

Performance, framing depth, finish and ceiling system change both material and labour.

02

Scale

Small packages carry proportionally more setup; larger repetitive areas usually price more efficiently.

03

Access

Working height, occupied spaces, restricted logistics and out-of-hours work increase delivery cost.

04

Interfaces

Openings, services, complex geometry and coordination with adjacent trades widen the planning range.
