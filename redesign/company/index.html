Company / Green Ways Group

A specialist focus. A clear identity.

GREENWAYS GROUP LTD is a UK specialist subcontractor working across suspended ceilings, structural framing systems, partitions and plastering for commercial interiors.

The company / The work

Commercial interiors.
Practical coordination.

GWG works with the package information that matters on site: the specified build-up, the line and level, the junction, the opening and the sequence.

Enquiries can begin during tender preparation or on a live project. The first step is to establish what is included and how the work connects to the wider project.

The website brings together our service scope, operating profile and real site photography. Detailed project credentials and the evidence required for an appointment should be reviewed directly with the company.
