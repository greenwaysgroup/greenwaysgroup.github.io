Green Ways Group installs suspended ceiling systems for commercial interiors, including exposed grid, concealed MF and other specified ceiling build-ups, coordinated with partitions, lighting, access points and building services.

Green Ways Group installs structural framing systems (SFS) in light-gauge steel to issued project designs, forming precise internal and external wall zones coordinated with the primary structure, boards, insulation and façade interfaces.

Green Ways Group installs commercial partitions, plasterboard linings and plastering packages, coordinating metal studs, boards, insulation, openings, service zones and specified fire, acoustic, moisture and finish requirements.

03 / Tender to site

Clear scope.
Considered sequence.

The useful conversation starts before installation: what is included, how it connects and when it can be built.

  1. 01Tender

    Define the package

    Start with drawings, specification, quantities and programme. Identify the system, the boundaries of the package and the information still to be agreed.

  2. 02Coordination

    Resolve the interfaces

    Review levels, openings, access and adjoining trades. Confirm the information needed at structure, services, façade and partition junctions before work is concealed.

  3. 03Site delivery

    Sequence the installation

    Align the agreed work with access, working areas and the project programme. Keep installation tied to the issued details and the selected system.
