Information about the contact tools and the content presented in this redesign.

Contact & data

Email and telephone

Contact links open your telephone or email application. An enquiry sent by email is received by GREENWAYS GROUP LTD. For questions about information you have sent, contact greenways@mail.co.uk.

Enquiry draft tool

The enquiry form assembles a draft in your browser. It does not submit the form to a server or save it in browser storage. You choose whether to open the draft in your email application and send it.

Cost estimator

The estimator calculates an indicative range in your browser. It is planning guidance, not a quotation or an offer. Drawings, specification, access, programme and site conditions must be reviewed before a price is agreed.

Website operation

This redesign does not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or marketing cookies. The hosting provider may process connection information to deliver the pages. External applications opened through contact links operate under their own terms and privacy information.
