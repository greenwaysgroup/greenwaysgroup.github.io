[QUESTIONS] Before you enquire

The details that
help define the package.

Can ceilings accommodate building services?

Yes. Lighting, ventilation, detectors and access points need to be coordinated against the reflected ceiling plan, system details and responsibilities of the relevant trades.

How is fire or acoustic performance established?

Performance belongs to the complete specified and evidenced system. It depends on the build-up, suspension, penetrations and junctions; it cannot be inferred from the finished surface alone.
