[QUESTIONS] Before you enquire

The details that
help define the package.

Can a package include curved partitions?

Curved construction can form part of the agreed scope where the layout, system and required finish are documented. The supplied GWG photography includes curved metal-stud and boarded work.

When should concealed interfaces be reviewed?

Before boarding and finishing. Head conditions, penetrations, insulation and service interfaces become harder to inspect once the assembly is closed.
