[INFORMATION] Privacy & site use

Your information.
A clear route to contact.

Enquiry drafts

The enquiry form prepares an email draft in your browser. The website does not submit the form to a server or send an email automatically. The draft is passed to your email app only when you select “Open email app”. You decide what to attach and when to send it.

Planning estimates

The cost estimator calculates in your browser. Values are not submitted to GWG by the estimator. Copying or downloading a brief creates a record for you to review and share through the contact route you choose.

Cookies and external services

This version of the site does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts use the fonts available on your device. The hosting provider may process ordinary web requests to deliver the pages. Google Maps links open an external website governed by its own privacy terms.

Information you send to GWG

Emailing or calling GWG shares the information you provide through that channel. To discuss information held in connection with a project enquiry, contact greenways@mail.co.uk or call 07492 005534.
