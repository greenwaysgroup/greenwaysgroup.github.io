Website information / Privacy

A direct, transparent enquiry route.

This page explains the behaviour of this website’s forms, calculator and external links.

Enquiry drafts

The contact form prepares an email draft in your browser. It does not submit the form to a website database. Choose to open your email application or copy the text, then review and send the enquiry yourself.

Information you send by email is received by GWG so the team can respond to your project or tender enquiry. For questions about information sent to GWG, visit the GWG contact page.

Budget calculator

Areas, rates and budget results are calculated in your browser. This implementation does not send those entries to a server or save them in browser storage. The entries remain temporary within the page.

Cookies and analytics

This implementation does not add analytics, marketing cookies or third-party tracking scripts. The site hosting provider may process ordinary request information to serve and protect the website.

External links

Links to Companies House, mapping services and technical references open external websites. Those services apply their own privacy policies.
