03 / Project evidence

The complete system counts

Fire, acoustic and structural performance depend on the assembly actually specified. Test or assessment evidence, manufacturer details and approved junctions must support the installed solution.

A regulatory reference is a technical framework, not a company certification.

The correct requirements depend on the building, location, approved design and any transitional provisions. Use the project’s current specification and responsible design team to establish the applicable requirements.
