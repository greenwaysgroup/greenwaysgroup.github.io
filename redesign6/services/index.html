[01] / Installation scope

Suspended ceilings

The ceiling is a working plane: it shapes the room, manages access to the void and meets the performance of the specified assembly. GWG installs the agreed ceiling build-up beneath the structural soffit.

  • Exposed T-grid systems with removable ceiling tiles
  • Concealed metal framework with plasterboard linings
  • Perimeters, bulkheads and access openings within the agreed package

Illustrative assembly. Issued design governs geometry, spacing and fixings.

Coordination

Confirm finished levels, plenum depth, lighting positions, service penetrations and access requirements before closing the void.

Performance

Use the selected system’s evidence for acoustic absorption, fire resistance and load capacity. Separate support may be needed for building services.

Information to issue

Reflected ceiling plans, ceiling types, sections, perimeter details and the schedule of access panels.

[02] / Installation scope

Structural framing systems

Light-gauge steel infill brings the issued wall design into the building frame. GWG’s installation scope is defined against the project engineer’s details, with particular care at slab edges, openings and façade interfaces.

  • Head and base channels, studs and local framing to issued details
  • Framed openings and interfaces with the primary structure
  • Specified sheathing and insulation where included in the package

Illustrative assembly. Issued design governs geometry, spacing and fixings.

Coordination

Check structural movements, fixing substrates, opening geometry and interfaces with the façade and waterproofing trades.

Performance

Section sizes, centres, restraints and fixings must follow the engineered design. Substitutions need design-team agreement.

Information to issue

SFS elevations, design calculations, connection details, fixing schedules, sheathing build-ups and tolerances.

[03] / Installation scope

Partitions and plastering

Walls need to suit the rooms they create. GWG installs metal stud partitions, drylining and specified plaster finishes, attending to the assembly behind the boards as carefully as the surface ready for decoration.

  • Metal stud and plasterboard partition assemblies
  • Wall linings, board joints and specified plaster finishes
  • Openings, local backing and acoustic infill to the issued design

Illustrative assembly. Issued design governs geometry, spacing and fixings.

Coordination

Confirm door sets, head deflection, service routes, backing for fixtures and junctions with adjacent construction.

Performance

Fire resistance, sound insulation and robustness depend on the complete tested build-up. Board layers and junction details matter.

Information to issue

Partition-type schedules, plans, elevations, performance requirements, door details and finish expectations.

Before the package is priced

Good questions.
Clearer scope.

Can GWG select a system from a performance target?

The project’s design team should confirm the required performance and approved assembly. GWG can discuss the installation package and the information needed to price and deliver it.

Does an SFS package include structural design?

Design responsibility is agreed in the scope. The installation presentation here is based on issued engineered details; it does not imply that structural design is included.

Are fire and acoustic ratings interchangeable?

No. A rating applies to a particular tested or assessed system and its detailing. Changing boards, insulation, fixings or junctions can change performance and needs review.

What is excluded from a budget allowance?

That depends on the agreed package. Clarify design fees, scaffolding, access equipment, specialist supports, waste removal, decoration and VAT before comparing allowances.
