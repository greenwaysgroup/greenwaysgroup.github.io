Partitions and Plastering

Partition, plasterboard and plastering work that turns a coordinated internal layout into defined rooms, linings and finished surfaces.

What the package does

Partitions divide internal space and provide the framework for plasterboard linings and finishes. A commercial drylining package may combine metal studs, tracks, insulation, boards, wall linings and details around doors, glazing, services and adjoining construction. The specified build-up determines requirements such as fire resistance, acoustic separation, moisture exposure and robustness. GWG installs partitions and plastering work within the agreed package; the required board types, layers, finish route and performance evidence come from the project specification and approved system details.

  • 01
    Forms internal partitions and wall linings to the documented layout and setting-out information.
  • 02
    Coordinates studs, boards, openings, service zones and junctions with the surrounding construction.
  • 03
    Prepares surfaces for the specified plaster, skim, tape-and-joint or other documented finish.
  • 04
    Keeps fire, acoustic, moisture and duty requirements connected to the complete tested or specified system.

Context / delivery

The system only works when its interfaces are understood.

Partitions and plasterboard linings are used throughout commercial fit-out and refurbishment work to divide spaces, line backgrounds and create finished internal surfaces. The package may include straight or curved construction, bulkheads and coordinated openings, but only where those items are shown in the agreed scope.

Useful information includes partition types, heights, board build-ups, duty and performance requirements, door and glazing details, service penetrations, head conditions and finish schedules. GWG installs against that coordinated information and the agreed sequence, with interfaces reviewed before boarding and finishing make them difficult to inspect.
