The package is a system

See the interfaces before they become site problems.

A ceiling, frame or partition does not exist alone. Each package meets structure, services, openings and adjacent work. The useful review is the relationship between them.

Levels, grid, hangers, perimeters, access and service penetrations are read as one ceiling package rather than isolated line items.

Tracks, studs, openings, insulation zones and structural interfaces are coordinated against the issued project information before lining closes them in.

Partition geometry connects setting-out, boards, insulation, openings, service zones and the finish that remains visible at handover.
