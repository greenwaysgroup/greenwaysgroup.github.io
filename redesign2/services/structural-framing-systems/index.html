Structural Framing Systems

Light-gauge steel framing installed to the project design, forming defined wall zones and interfaces within the wider building assembly.

What the package does

Structural framing systems, commonly referred to as SFS, use light-gauge steel sections to form a precise framed construction. Infill framing can sit between the floors and soffits of the primary structure, while other arrangements may be positioned around the structural frame. GWG installs SFS where it is included in the agreed package and where suitable design information is issued. Structural design, engineering, system selection, supply, warranty and façade responsibility remain exactly as allocated by the contract and project team.

  • 01
    Forms the specified light-gauge steel frame at internal or external wall zones shown in the project design.
  • 02
    Establishes accurate lines, openings and connection points for the documented wall build-up.
  • 03
    Coordinates with the primary structure and with adjoining board, insulation and façade interfaces.
  • 04
    Provides a buildable installation only when the design responsibility and system information are clear.

Context / delivery

The system only works when its interfaces are understood.

SFS is commonly used where a lightweight, accurately set-out frame is required around the main structure, including external wall infill and other defined framing applications. Its relationship with deflection, floor edges, openings, sheathing, insulation and façade systems makes interface information particularly important.

Before installation, the package needs coordinated drawings, structural and system details, setting-out information, connection requirements and clear responsibility for the complete wall build-up. GWG works to the issued design and agreed scope. Ambiguous or changed interfaces should be resolved before they are concealed or passed to the following trade.
