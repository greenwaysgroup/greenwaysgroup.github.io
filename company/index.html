Standards and evidence

The documents behind due diligence.

Main contractors may need current evidence across accreditation, certification, insurance, health and safety and competence. The structure below is ready for exact records without inventing credentials or displaying expired information as current.

01

Accreditations

Current accreditation details can be listed after the issuer, scope, reference and validity dates are supported and approved.

02

Certifications

Current certificates can be presented when the issuing body, certified scope, reference and dates are verified from the source document.

03

Competencies

Competence records need a defined category, awarding body, holder or scope, validity information and a completed privacy review.

04

Health & safety

Health and safety information can be added when the current document, owner, revision date and publication approval are available.

05

Insurance

Insurance information requires a current policy or schedule, an approved summary of cover and a clear decision on what may be published.
