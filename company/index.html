Operating principles

TECHNICAL LANGUAGE WITHOUT OVERCLAIMING RESPONSIBILITY.

A subcontractor website should help a project team understand what is installed and what information is needed, without silently assuming design, engineering, warranty or performance obligations that belong elsewhere in the contract.

Principle[01]

Defined scope

Package inclusions, exclusions and interfaces should be visible enough to discuss before work starts.

Principle[02]

Issued information

Installation follows drawings, specifications, schedules and approved details relevant to the agreed work.

Principle[03]

Evidence boundaries

Performance and project claims stay connected to verifiable records rather than being inferred from photographs or generic product language.
