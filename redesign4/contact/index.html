Useful details

What to include in a first enquiry

A short, useful brief helps establish whether the package and timing are relevant. Include the information available now; detailed documents can follow through the agreed contact route.

  • Project name and location
  • Relevant ceiling, SFS, partition or plastering package
  • Current project or tender stage
  • Programme or return dates
  • Your company, role and contact details

There is no portal or upload process on this site. Use the published phone number or email address and include enough context for a focused first conversation.
